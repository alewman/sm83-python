## Author respect (2026-11-26)

Kept apart from the licence findings above: this is respect, not law. furrtek objects to AI use
of his work, and the family sets his work and its lineage aside for that wish (Aubrey,
2026-11-26; emulator-lineage `docs/sources.md` at `d9b8ce2`, the "set aside (author's wishes)"
verdict and the Excluded row). The lineage that touched this repository:

- **msinger's dmg-sim** (`786f8c8a`): SystemVerilog generated from msinger's dmg-schematics,
  whose DMG-CPU B schematics "were derived from the schematics in furrtek/DMG-CPU-Inside" (its
  README); its `sm83/` is msinger's own but lives in the same netlist, so it goes with it.
- **GateBoy / metroboy** (`36797ad4`): a gate-level port of furrtek's schematics; its README,
  `docs/` and `notes/` were pinned and read.

**History, plainly.** The earlier documents said furrtek's own repository was not used; that
was true of DMG-CPU-Inside itself, but his lineage was used. dmg-sim was fetched, built and run
as the second die model from 2026-10-05 (finding 1) to 2026-11-26: it ran Mooneye `daa`, the
all-opcodes ROM (533 cases) and 24 timer-woken HALT cases, and commit `e2e10cb` (2026-10-07)
changed the core from it: a halted CPU drives `$0000` (Gekkio's VHDL shows the same) and wakes
in the cycle after IF rises with no idle cycle (dmg-sim alone). GateBoy's README was read for
the eight clock phases and for its CPU being a guess (C6); its source was never used here. On
2026-11-26: both pins left `scripts/pinned_repos.json`; `scripts/dmgsim_run.py`,
`dmgsim_rows.py`, `dmgsim_halt.py` and `scripts/dmgsim/bus_probe.sv` left the tree (copies in
`/data/backup/sm83-python-furrtek-lineage-2026-11-26/moved-from-repo-at-f827a64/`);
`build_referees.py` lost its `dmgsim` step, `die_compare.py` its `--dmgsim` option,
`tests/test_die.py` its two dmg-sim tests; the fetched trees `reference/src/dmg-sim` (with its
build) and `reference/src/metroboy` moved unopened to
`/data/backup/sm83-python-furrtek-lineage-2026-11-26/reference/src/`. No behaviour changed.

**The die rung (5) becomes one tracing:** Gekkio's VHDL SM83, traced from his own photographs
of an SGB-CPU 01 die (`gb-research/sm83-cpu-core/README.markdown`); nothing in gb-research or
GBCTR names furrtek, his schematics, msinger's or GateBoy (searched 2026-11-26). The wake-up,
which the mock SoC cannot show, moves to rung 6's test ROMs.
